Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Lift Legends handles information when you use the Game, including its online multiplayer features, account system, camera-based AI features, and in-game purchases.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

01

Information We Collect

Lift Legends collects only the information necessary to provide the Game's core functionality.

Game Login Identifiers
Used to authenticate players and associate their gameplay progress with their game account.
Gameplay Data
Includes player progress and other information required to provide the Game's functionality.
No real-world identifying information is collected. Lift Legends does not require your real name, physical address, phone number, or similar real-world identifying information to play the Game.
02

Cloud Database

Lift Legends uses a cloud database hosted on Microsoft Azure.

The database stores game login identifiers and player progress and gameplay data.

Login information is used only to authenticate players and associate gameplay progress with their game account.

We do not use this information for advertising or profiling users.

03

Online Multiplayer

Lift Legends includes online multiplayer functionality.

Multiplayer services are hosted using Amazon Web Services (AWS) and use the Mirror networking framework.

During multiplayer sessions, gameplay-related information may be transmitted between players and game servers in order to provide the multiplayer experience.

No personal or real-world identifying information is collected during multiplayer sessions.

04

Profile Pictures

Lift Legends allows players to select or create a profile picture.

Profile pictures are not stored in our cloud database.

The selected profile picture is stored locally on the player's device. When a player joins a multiplayer server, the profile picture may be temporarily transmitted to and stored on the server machine so that it can be displayed to other players.

When the player leaves the server, the server deletes the stored profile picture.

05

Camera & AI Gym Detection

Lift Legends uses the device camera for an AI-powered feature that detects objects in an image to determine whether the player is in a gym environment.

For example, the system may analyze objects such as dumbbells, barbells, treadmills, exercise bikes, or other gym-related equipment.

Images used for this feature are not saved. They are processed for the purpose of the detection feature and are not stored in the cloud database.

We do not use these images for advertising, profiling, facial recognition, or any other unrelated purpose.

06

In-Game Currency & Purchases

Lift Legends includes an in-game currency system.

In-game currency may be used to purchase virtual gameplay items, including potions and chests.

Players may also have the ability to purchase changes or customization options related to their profile picture.

Virtual items and currency are used only within the Game and do not represent real-world monetary value outside the Game.

07

How We Use Information

Information collected by Lift Legends is used only for purposes necessary to operate and improve the Game.

  • Authenticating game accounts.
  • Saving and restoring player progress.
  • Providing online multiplayer functionality.
  • Providing gameplay features and virtual items.
  • Operating the camera-based AI gym detection feature.
  • Maintaining and securing the Game's services.

We do not use collected information for targeted advertising or unrelated profiling.

08

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the Game.

These providers may include cloud hosting and database services such as Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services.

Such services are used to provide the technical infrastructure required for the Game.

09

Children's Privacy

Lift Legends may be played by users under the age of 13.

The Game does not knowingly require children to provide real-world identifying information such as their name, physical address, or phone number.

Lift Legends is a non-violent game.

The information collected by the Game is limited to what is necessary for account authentication, gameplay, multiplayer functionality, and other core Game features.

Parents or guardians who have questions about information associated with a child's account may contact us using the contact information provided below.

10

Account Deletion

Players may delete their account at any time.

When an account is deleted, the account and associated gameplay data will no longer be available for use in the Game.

Important: Deleting your account means that your saved gameplay progress and associated account data will be lost.
11

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used to operate Lift Legends and to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12

Third-Party Services

Lift Legends relies on third-party infrastructure providers to operate certain services.

  • Microsoft Azure — cloud database infrastructure.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — multiplayer server infrastructure.
  • Mirror — networking framework used for online multiplayer functionality.

These services may process information as necessary to provide the infrastructure used by the Game.

13

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

14

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the Lift Legends development team.
